# Known Issues / Boundaries — v0.7

- Actual Android hardware and human usability are not tested by Chromium emulation.
- `file://` persistence is scoped by browser/profile/origin and subject to quota/eviction. Text Backup is required before moving files/profiles.
- Whole Campaign Text includes historical simulation graphs. Creation/restore may take several seconds on slower machines; UI displays progress state. The structure favors reviewability over compact size.
- Package DC power/traffic/time keep original boundaries. Substrate results affect Chapter5 simulation, Mission acceptance and rewards; they do not silently change legacy96W Package accounting.
- Gaussian alignment and fixed-loss interface presets are educational and uncalibrated. No actual evanescent solver, process yield, reliability, BER or vendor-specific qualification.
- Advanced Coupling Workshop explores existing Chapter5 parameters. Chapter6 gameplay is a future release.
- The original Advanced Lab and its storage remain independent; full Campaign Backup does not include its Design data.
