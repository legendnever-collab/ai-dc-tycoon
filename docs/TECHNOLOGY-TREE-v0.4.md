# Technology Tree — v0.4.0

## Existing Chapter 01 progression (retained)
- Starter Compute Rack → Rack Expansion → Scale-out Switch Upgrade → Network Analyzer.
- Pluggable Preview and planned CPO/Optical Engine/Photonic Substrate remain in the old Source Catalog for legacy parity.

## Added Chapter 02 unlock sequence
- Mission 03 Clear ⇒ **Switch Port Crisis** unlocks.
- Mission 04 Clear ⇒ **High-Density Switch** know-how, **Link Builder**, Mission 05 available.
- Mission 05 Clear ⇒ **Spine / Leaf–Spine Topology** learning, Mission 06 available.
- Mission 06 Clear ⇒ **800G Pluggable**, **Optical Link Analyzer** (actual rate/path/power inspection), **CPO Research Preview** (qualitative only).
- Future Release v0.5 ⇒ actual CPO comparison and technology strategy missions, not part of v0.4.

Rewards/XP do not affect physical Link Rate or Network capacity. Only installed components and active compatible Link Paths change these simulation outputs. Chapter 02 unlock receipts are independent of the frozen Chapter 01 Technology Tree to retain compatibility with v0.3 profiles.

The top Technology Tree shows Chapter2 cards derived from real receipts. Equipment is available in the guided scenario where it must be learned; the reward records ownership/know-how and unlocks the next mission. HD/Spine/800G selections remain usable on replay. Rewards never multiply physical performance.
